## Overview

PivotAI Career Platform is a comprehensive career development system that combines AI-powered roadmap generation, gamification elements, and social features to help professionals advance their careers. The platform supports multiple professional fields including technology, business, engineering, medicine, and law.

---

## Authentication & User Roles

### Firebase Authentication
- **Email/Password Login**: Secure authentication with email verification
- **OAuth Providers**: Support for Google, GitHub, and other social logins
- **Role-Based Access Control**: 
  - Candidates: Job seekers and career changers
  - Recruiters: Talent acquisition professionals
  - Admins: Platform administrators

### User Profiles
- **Professional Information**: Name, current role, experience level
- **Field Selection**: Choose from technology, business, engineering, medicine, or law
- **Resume Management**: Upload and parse resumes for automated analysis
- **Target Companies**: Track desired employers and positions

## Core Features

### 1. AI-Powered Career Roadmap Generation

#### Resume Analysis
- **Automated Parsing**: Extract skills, experience, and education from uploaded resumes
- **Skill Identification**: Recognize technical and soft skills
- **Gap Analysis**: Identify missing skills for target positions
- **Strengths & Weaknesses**: AI-powered assessment of professional profile

#### Roadmap Creation
- **Personalized Milestones**: Custom career milestones based on:
  - Current skills and experience
  - Target companies and positions
  - Professional field
  - Career goals
- **Progressive Leveling**: Milestones organized into 10 levels of difficulty
- **Time Estimates**: Realistic timeframes for each milestone
- **Resource Recommendations**: Curated learning resources for each milestone

#### Milestone Categories
- **Technical Skills**: Field-specific technical competencies
- **Fundamental Skills**: Core professional abilities
- **Soft Skills**: Communication, leadership, teamwork
- **Career Advancement**: Networking, interviews, portfolio building
- **Niche Specializations**: Advanced field-specific expertise

### 2. Task Management System

#### Milestone Tasks
- **Subtask Breakdown**: Each milestone contains actionable tasks
- **Progress Tracking**: Mark tasks as complete
- **Prerequisites**: Unlock advanced milestones by completing basics
- **Difficulty Indicators**: Clear difficulty ratings (1-5)

#### Micro-Milestones
- **Quick Wins**: Smaller achievements between major milestones
- **Daily Goals**: Maintain momentum with daily objectives
- **Skill Reinforcement**: Practice and reinforce learned skills

## Gamification System

### 1. Leveling System
- **Starting Point**: All users begin at Level 1
- **Progression**: Complete 5 milestones to advance to the next level
- **Level Cap**: Progress through 10 levels of mastery
- **Visual Indicators**: Progress bars and level badges

### 2. Achievement System

#### Achievement Categories
- **Progress Achievements**: Milestone completion rewards
- **Streak Achievements**: Consistency-based rewards
- **Skill Achievements**: Mastery of specific competencies
- **Special Achievements**: Hidden and event-based rewards

#### Achievement Rarities
- Common (Gray)
- Uncommon (Green)
- Rare (Blue)
- Epic (Purple)
- Legendary (Gold)

### 3. Streak System
- **Daily Streaks**: Track consecutive days of activity
- **Streak Rewards**: Unlock bonuses at 3, 7, 14, 30, 50, and 100 days
- **Learning Boost**: Multipliers increase with longer streaks
- **Visual Tracking**: Flame icons and calendar heat maps

### 4. Character Classes (RPG Elements)
- **Tech Wizard**: Intelligence and wisdom bonuses
- **Business Paladin**: Strength and charisma bonuses
- **Creative Bard**: Charisma and dexterity bonuses
- **Data Ranger**: Intelligence and dexterity bonuses
- **Marketing Alchemist**: Charisma and wisdom bonuses
- **Finance Monk**: Intelligence and constitution bonuses
